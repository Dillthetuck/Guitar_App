## Proposed slice acceptance checklist

- Correct deterministic note answers against independently specified fixtures; repeated taps and mistakes do not duplicate progress.
- Help and reveal are recorded by actual exposure; independent responses stay distinct from corrected/revealed responses.
- Fresh questions vary order and include naming as well as locating; skipping changes access without awarding mastery.
- Saved progress survives agreed reopen/refresh scenarios; missing/corrupt saves fail safely; reset/export and version changes have defined behavior.
- Portrait phone interactions are comfortable on actual target devices; no accidental adjacent-fret selection pattern; compare interaction alternatives before locking the board.
- Keyboard, screen reader, non-color feedback, readable text, reduced motion and sound mute are verified in the chosen implementation.
- Audio uses cleared real-guitar samples with pitch/octave identifiers; actual device playback, interruption and mute behavior are checked.

Freeze measurable criteria and device list before building. These are proposed checks, not claims of passed acceptance.

## Proposed small player study

Recruit about five target players as a usability pilot, with explicit consent and minimal identifying data. This can find problems; it cannot establish population-level effectiveness or market demand. Agent does not contact people without authorization.

1. Brief baseline locating/naming check without teaching; record prior guitar knowledge.
2. Give lesson without coaching. Record start/completion, actual duration, wrong taps, hints/reveals, confusion and interventions.
3. Use fresh independent questions after the lesson. Preserve first responses; do not count correction as independent success.
4. Follow up after an agreed delay, proposed 24–48 hours, without a recap first. Record recruitment reminders separately from voluntary returns.
5. For participants with a guitar, ask them to locate/play requested notes with labels hidden; record this separately from screen performance.
6. Ask what they would choose to practice next and why. Compare with actual return behavior; enthusiasm alone is not retention.

Agree question count, comparable item sets, retention interval, timing window and continue/revise criteria with Dylan before running the study. Investigate any consistent usability barrier or hint dependence before bulk content. Do not use the pilot as proof of broad efficacy, conversion or profitability.
