November 6, 2026. Authorized by Dylan's “ok continue” following the proposed focused landscape design specimen. This is a design/interaction sample, not approval or implementation of the full product milestone.

## What was prepared

An original six-string neck drawn in blue on pale yellow with controlled line texture, visible nut/frets/markers, progressively thicker strings, open-position input, and one active string. A small loop samples low-E F/G finding and F naming, then A-string B/C finding and B naming. This is interaction content, not the complete teaching route in FIRST-TOPIC-SPEC.md.

Includes correct feedback, wrong-pitch correction, direct requested help, assisted-success wording, next-question progression and a leave/return overlay. No sound, complete path/navigation, accounts, daily scheduling or production persistence is included. Host state restoration is for the review specimen only. The font candidate is Kalam with plain functional supporting text; final typeface/rights review and visual approval remain open. Palette rendering is provisional against the original profiled references.

Difficulty stays Foundations/pale yellow throughout the sample. It does not turn red-orange with lesson completion. Texture and string-weight settings are exposed through optional host design controls.
