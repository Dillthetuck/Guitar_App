# Open practice with optional teaching

November 6, 2026. Lead-owned planning specification. Dylan's stated product direction is recorded below; detailed behavior is proposed. No implementation or expanded release scope is approved.

## Confirmed direction

- Higher-level exercises remain accessible without mandatory course completion or testing.
- The product also supports a learner who does not yet know note names.
- Each overarching exercise area should offer an optional guided lesson series or walkthrough, followed by endless reinforcement practice.
- Entry must be easy, while daily practice should remain compelling.

These statements concern learning access. They do not settle whether all content is free of charge, premium boundaries, or the first release's full topic coverage. Beginner theory support does not automatically add guitar technique, microphone grading or a full learn-to-play course.

## Proposed topic structure

One topic page offers:

| Entry | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Learn this | Short sequence teaching prerequisites and the target concept; can be entered, resumed or left without gating practice |
| Practice now | Begin from a sensible preset; optional controls narrow strings, frets, notes or difficulty |
| Quick check | Optional assessment to recommend an entry point or review; never required for access |

The player's teaching and practice use the same underlying music definitions, so a lesson introduces exactly what practice asks. Prompt type and help vary: a lesson may show a worked example, while practice begins without the answer shown. Returning to help records assisted work rather than inventing independent success.

## Beginner note journey: representative low-E/A slice

1. Choose Fretboard notes, then Learn this. No placement test required.
2. Briefly establish string orientation, open-string meaning and what a fret represents. These are optional/resumable explanations, not an onboarding questionnaire.
3. Introduce open E and show E–F as a one-fret move. Include a worked example before asking the learner to retrieve it.
4. Guide a find-F activity, then ask naming and finding questions with answer labels removed. Separate exposure, help and first-response evidence.
5. Offer practice using only the introduced scope by default. The player may expand scope at any time with a clear explanation of what changes.
6. Offer a short stopping point and an optional Continue practicing action. Endless practice remains available; finite daily completion provides a satisfying endpoint without blocking more work.
7. Recommend a useful next session using prior mistakes/help and introduced concepts. Actual scheduling rules and thresholds still need specification.

An experienced player chooses Practice now and bypasses this sequence. A beginner who enters advanced material can request a small prerequisite explanation or choose a simpler scope without being locked out.

## Daily pull proposal

Recommend one small session that mixes encountered concepts needing review with current learning. Show a meaningful result, such as items answered independently and a next step. Preserve a clear finish and allow unlimited continuation. Streaks can recognize participation, but cannot claim mastery or dictate which topic the player must choose.

Avoid making beginners design their own exercise settings before the first question. Provide level/scope presets and optional customization. “Endless” describes availability, not an obligation to continue indefinitely.

## Topic expansion pattern

Long-term candidate families: notes, intervals, chords/triads and scales. For each, author an introductory route, representative worked examples, graded independent tasks, correction/help and practice presets. These families are a product map, not next-week release content. Do not generate a broad curriculum before one family is tested.

Original low-E/A coverage remains the smallest sensible prototype candidate for proving both entry routes. Exact lesson count, complete teaching script, sound, device layout, architecture and revised schedule remain open. Earlier rejected visual concepts remain rejected.

## Proposed acceptance evidence for later build approval

- A learner without note-name knowledge can understand the first guided task without external coaching; record actual confusion and interventions.
- A more experienced player can reach a chosen practice activity directly, with no compulsory test or prior lesson.
- Practice after teaching stays within its stated default scope; widening it is explicit.
- Help can be requested and teaching resumed without losing the practice context; history distinguishes assisted and independent work.
- The daily session has an honest finish and an easy continuation path. Neither choosing advanced material nor completing guided taps awards mastery.
- Use actual fresh/delayed questions and eventual guitar tasks to evaluate learning separately from interface completion.

These are draft behavioral criteria, not observed results. Next planning work is one complete topic specification and first-use/return flow before another aesthetic exploration or product implementation.
